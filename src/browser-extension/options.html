Markport Clipper

Saves images you click on web pages into a vault: one open in desktop Obsidian, or one open in a Markport tab. Either way the vault needs the Markport - Image Clipper plugin.

Obsidian (desktop app)

Pairing keys, one per line. Find a vault's key in Obsidian under Settings > Markport - Image Clipper > Pairing key.

Markport

Addresses where you open Markport, one per line.

Only the origin counts (scheme, host and port): https://notes.example.com/vault/abc is stored as https://notes.example.com.

Dragging

Takes effect on pages opened or reloaded after the change.
